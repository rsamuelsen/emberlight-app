# Installing Emberlight Companion on Windows

Works on Windows 10 and 11. You don't need administrator rights.

## 1. Download

Download **[Emberlight-Companion-Setup.exe](https://github.com/rsamuelsen/emberlight-app/releases/latest/download/Emberlight-Companion-Setup.exe)**.
It's always the newest version. Your browser may ask whether to keep the file: choose **Keep**.

## 2. Run the installer

Open the file you downloaded (it's usually in your **Downloads** folder).

Windows will probably show a blue box saying **"Windows protected your PC"**. That's because
Emberlight is a volunteer project without a paid Microsoft certificate, not because anything is
wrong. To continue:

1. Click **More info** (the small link under the text).
2. Click **Run anyway**.

The installer then puts Emberlight Companion on your computer and opens it.

## 3. The four first steps

The Companion opens with four short steps. You can skip any of them and finish later.

1. **World of Warcraft folder.** It usually finds the game by itself and says **Found**. If not,
   click **Choose folder** and pick the folder called `World of Warcraft`.
2. **Emberlight addon.** Click **Install**. You'll see the addon in game the next time you log in.
3. **Discord account.** Click **Sign in**. Your browser opens Discord's own page: use the Discord
   account you have on the Emberlight server, allow it, then come back to the Companion.
4. **Your character.** Link the character you play. The website opens and explains each step.

That's it. Press **Play** whenever you like.

## Good to know

- Closing the window keeps the Companion running in the tray, beside the clock, so it can tell you
  about new guild events. To quit, right-click the Emberlight icon there and choose **Quit**. You
  can change this in Settings.
- When there's a new version, the Companion offers **Restart to update**. You never need to
  download it again.
- What it reads and sends: [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
- To remove it: Windows **Settings**, **Apps**, find **Emberlight Companion**, **Uninstall**.

## Just the addon

You can use the addon without the Companion, on any computer:

1. Download **[Emberlight-addon.zip](https://github.com/rsamuelsen/emberlight-app/releases/latest/download/Emberlight-addon.zip)**.
2. Close World of Warcraft.
3. Open your `World of Warcraft` folder, then the folder for the game version you play (during
   the WoW: Forever beta that's `_classic_beta_`), then `Interface`, then `AddOns`. Create
   `Interface` and `AddOns` if they aren't there.
4. Right-click the zip, choose **Extract All**, and extract it into that `AddOns` folder. You should
   end up with `AddOns\Emberlight\Emberlight.toc`.
5. Start the game. Open Emberlight with the guild crest by the minimap, or type `/emberlight`.

Without the Companion you won't see posts made while you were offline, and you update the addon
yourself by repeating these steps.
